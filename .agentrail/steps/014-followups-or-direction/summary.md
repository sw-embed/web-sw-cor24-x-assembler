Fixed the 'battery on but clock never advances' bug. The previous attach matrix returned all-zero regs when battery was on but no persisted state existed, and the Read demo never writes to the bus so the synchronization trap had nothing to persist -- battery-on was indistinguishable from battery-off until the user explicitly ran I2C RTC Set. Now: when battery is on and load() is None, save a fresh {00:00:00, now} anchor before attaching. effective_now() then computes elapsed-time advance on subsequent Runs and page reloads. A 5-minute gap shows 00:05:00 second time around. Set To System Time and the i2c-write sync trap are unchanged; they overwrite the anchor with their own values. clippy -D warnings green; pages/ rebuilt.
